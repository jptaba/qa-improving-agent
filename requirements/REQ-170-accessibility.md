## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
- **AC1:** THE SYSTEM SHALL have no automatically detectable WCAG 2.2 A/AA violations on every page, including the sign-in, checkout, order confirmation and "page not found" pages.
- **AC2:** THE SYSTEM SHALL give every form field an accessible name.

## Notes
- Automated checks catch only part of real accessibility issues (Deque reports ~57% by volume). Keep the manual checklist in Phase 3.
